Mobile Data Protection

By providing your mobile number, you consent to receive text messages from House Conservatives. We are currently establishing our SMS program; when our program launches, message frequency will vary and you may receive recurring messages related to our work. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help.

No Sale of Mobile Data

In compliance with A2P and 10DLC regulations, your mobile phone number will not be sold, shared, or rented to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-In Consent

By opting in to text messaging, you consent to receive recurring messages from House Conservatives once our SMS program launches. Opt out anytime by replying STOP or contacting contact@house-conservatives.com.
